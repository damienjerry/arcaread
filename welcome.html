FocusRead

Read the web faster. Bolded prefixes, clean reader view, on-device summaries.

What you just got

B

Bionic bolding on every page

The first part of each word is bolded so your eye skims faster. Adjust intensity and word-length threshold per site from the popup.

R

Reader view for any article

From the popup, click Open this page in Reader to strip the page down to its content with bionic applied, TTS playback, and phrase-chunking speed-read mode.

P

PDFs too

Flip Open PDFs in reader in the popup and every PDF you click opens in the FocusRead reader — clean text with bionic formatting instead of a black PDF viewer.

S

On-device AI summary

In Reader, click Summarize for a TL;DR generated by Chrome's built-in Gemini Nano. Runs locally, costs nothing, leaks nothing.

A

Accessibility toolkit

Open the Dyslexia mode section in the popup for line-height, letter-spacing, word-spacing and an optional Lexend reading font — tuned per site.

Keyboard shortcut

Press Cmd+Shift+F (or Ctrl+Shift+F on Windows / Linux) on any page to toggle FocusRead for that site.
